Passkeys

Register a passkey — a FIDO2 / WebAuthn credential (a hardware security key like a YubiKey, SoloKey, or Titan, or an Apple/Windows device passkey) — to skip the OTP step on login. Touch the key or authenticator when prompted; the credential is bound to this site by your browser and can't be used elsewhere.

OTP login keeps working alongside passkeys — pick “Send OTP instead” on the login page if your passkey is unavailable.
